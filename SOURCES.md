## Read this before using the route

The selected country sequence is a **game scenario** assembled from evidenced industrial activities. None of the records below proves that these companies form one connected supplier chain, or that this route manufactures a particular iPhone part.

Use sources at the scope they support. A company product page establishes a capability; a site page establishes a location. Neither alone proves a shipment, a customer contract, or that every process shown happens at that site.

Do not mark a claim “fact” merely because it sounds familiar. Store the supported statement and its limits. Parameters, travel times, batch sizes and gameplay outcomes remain game assumptions unless separately evidenced.
