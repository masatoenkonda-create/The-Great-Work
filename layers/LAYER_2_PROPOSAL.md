# Procurement layer — candidate for discussion

Codex, 22 October 2026. **Proposal, not approved for implementation.** Rationale and replies: [brainstorm turn 2](LAYER_2_BRAINSTORM.md#turn-2-codex-22-october-2026). [Evidence and limits](sources/README.md).

**Takeaway:** “I must secure inputs that work together, in usable quantities, from suppliers who can actually supply them.”

**Four questions:** the workshop reveals why an input is needed; a supplier branch reveals its source; ordering, acceptance and loading reveal how it is obtained/used; the same sample/bag/bottle/spool makes its physical form clear.

**Route:** keep seven main stops. A short supplier branch rejoins its own workshop. Keep the tracked silicon → wafer → chip → TV; purchase additional inputs and services rather than rebuying the player's existing output.

| Beat | Action and visible consequence |
|---|---|
| Need | Identify one missing material and the job it does in the machine |
| Source | Compare two offers; inspect a sample or request evidence |
| Commit | Choose suitable supply, usable quantity and order total |
| Accept | Check received object, lot and quantity against the order |
| Use | Feed it into the workshop and observe the result; retain the lot link |

**Distinct challenges:** Norway feed preparation/usable quantity; Taiwan accepted life of chemical supply; China paste/stencil matching. Germany process-service sourcing, Japan crucible fit and Malaysia package-material compatibility are candidates needing focused evidence. USA reconciles the run; no artificial seventh purchasing puzzle. Use one substantial new decision at each production stop, with routine auxiliary supplies bundled.

**First paper walkthrough:** Norway establishes the branch-and-rejoin flow. China demonstrates how a sourcing decision changes physical work: test-print candidate paste on a removable coupon before committing the real board. Source-specific material and geometry must be selected before building.

**Cost:** show wallet/debt plus current order total. Internally distinguish cash, commitments, consumed inputs/services and unused stock. Use declared toy credits; no researched country percentages or real TV price established. Charge bounded represented batches/attempts, never time spent reading or away from the tab. No generic cheapness-to-failure score, random defect penalty or double-billed electricity.

**Progression:** run 1 is a shorter guided transformation; run 2 adds input selection/preparation/acceptance. Later shipping/compliance runs expose their own dependencies. Guides perform omitted real work; matter never appears from nowhere. Compress mastered unchanged actions while preserving the new interaction and its consequence. Keep existing full workshops as the reference.

**Failure:** user decision: money running out permits continued play and a bad result at home. Proposal: a clearly fictional debt account and retry of the current loop, retaining earlier tutorial completion. Debt never changes an electrical test result. Exact restart/save rules remain to design.
