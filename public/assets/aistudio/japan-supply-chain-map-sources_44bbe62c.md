## Questions for substantive discussion
These are analytical prompts, not sourced conclusions:
- Does qualification protect a supplier through technical uniqueness, switching risk, or both?
- Does adding fabs to Japan increase local bargaining power or largely increase orders for incumbent global suppliers?
- Which inputs recur with wafer output, which depend on new-fab capex, and which benefit from packaging complexity?
- What would actually have to be local for a fab to keep operating through a disruption elsewhere?
- For a coordinator, where do qualification, change control, customs, spare-parts availability and lead times turn a purchase order into a production risk?
